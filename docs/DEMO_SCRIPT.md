# Demo script (about 20 minutes)

Sign in as `owner` on a big screen. Load the sample data first (`seed_catalog`), or the client's real parts if you have them.
Ask the client to speak up whenever something differs from how they work today. Write down every comment.

## 1. The owner's view (3 min)
- Open **Dashboard**. "This is what you see when you open the system in the morning."
- Point at today's sales, who owes money, what is low in stock, and stock value.

## 2. Find a part fast (3 min)
- **Items**: search by part number, then by an alternate number, then by a few words from the name.
- Filter by brand and vehicle make. Tick **Low stock only**.
- Ask: "Which of these columns do you actually use? What do you search by most?"

## 3. Receive goods (3 min)
- **Purchase** > **+ New receipt**. Pick a supplier, add two parts, enter received quantities and costs.
- Show a short delivery being flagged. Click **Post GRN**, then show the stock went up on **Items**.
- Ask: "Do you order from suppliers on paper, WhatsApp, or phone? Do you want purchase orders?"

## 4. Bill a customer (5 min)
- **Billing**: press `F2`, add parts, pick a garage, choose **Credit**, enter an amount received, press `F9`.
- Show the printed GST invoice and the WhatsApp button.
- Show rules: sign in as `counter` in another window and try a 20% discount and a price change. Both are refused.
- Return one item and show stock going back up.
- Ask: "Are your shelf prices with or without GST? What discount can staff give?"

## 5. Collect money (3 min)
- **Customers**: show the balances and the ageing buckets, then open the garage.
- **Receive payment**, then send a WhatsApp reminder to another customer.
- Ask: "How do you chase payments today? Who is allowed to take them?"

## 6. Reports (3 min)
- **Sales Report** as the owner: change the period, show top parts and brands, **Export to Excel**.
- Ask: "Which report would you look at every day? Do you need profit per part?"

## Questions to leave with the client
1. Which screen would you use first, and which would you never use?
2. What is missing from the day-to-day work (returns to suppliers, warranty, vehicle lookups, multiple shops)?
3. How many staff will use it at the same time, and on what devices?
4. Do you need e-invoicing or an e-way bill, or a Tally export for your accountant?
5. Can you share your real Excel sheets (parts, customers with balances, suppliers)?
